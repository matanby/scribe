# Scribe 1.0.1

- Choose Automatic, Right to left, or Left to right from the Aa menu for selected paragraphs and checklist items.
- Manual text direction survives editing, undo/redo, and reopening notes. New checklist items inherit the chosen direction.
- Mixed Hebrew and English checklist items keep their checkboxes on the chosen side, including items beginning with English words.
- Notes remain Markdown files; manually directed blocks use standard HTML direction markup.
